title: Double opt-in
description: Sign-ups that wait until the contact confirms their address (Plunk Lite)

---

With the double opt-in on, a sign-up from the API does not subscribe the contact. Plunk emails them a link, and only when they open it and press **Confirm** does the contact become subscribed. Until then the contact shows as **Pending confirmation**: they receive no campaigns and no marketing emails from workflows, and workflows that start on `contact.subscribed` do not run for them.

It is a Plunk Lite feature, off by default, set per project in **Settings → Brand → Double opt-in**.

## Turning it on

1. Choose the **From address** of the confirmation emails, on one of the project's verified domains, and optionally a name.
2. Edit the email if you want: a subject, a text and a button, per language. Languages without a text of their own use Plunk's. `{projectName}` becomes the project's name; template tags (`{{ … }}`, `{% … %}`) are not allowed.
3. Switch it on and save.

The email goes to each contact in their language: their `locale` field, else the project's language. It carries no unsubscribe footer. It is sent as a transactional email, so it counts as one and follows the project's open tracking; its link is never click-tracked, so the confirmation link is not kept in an event.

## What waits for confirmation

| Way in | With the double opt-in on |
| --- | --- |
| `POST /v1/track` with the secret key, creating a contact or with `subscribed: true` | Waits, and the confirmation email goes out |
| `POST /v1/track` with the public key | No email: the contact stays as it was (a new one unsubscribed) |
| `POST /contacts` with an API key | Waits, and the confirmation email goes out |
| `POST /v1/send` with `subscribed: true` | Waits, and the confirmation email goes out besides the email sent. A marketing template is refused with `422 OPT_IN_NEEDS_TRANSACTIONAL`, before anything is written |
| The public subscribe page, without a link from an email the contact received | Only for a contact already waiting to confirm: the confirmation email goes out again and the page asks them to check their inbox. Anyone else is refused with `409` `CONFIRMATION_NEEDS_EMAIL`: the page needs only the contact's id, which the public key hands out |
| A workflow's **Update contact** step that subscribes | Only for a contact already waiting to confirm: the confirmation email goes out again. Anyone else is left unsubscribed, and the step's result says `optIn: "held"` |
| A CSV import | New contacts are subscribed; unsubscribed ones stay unsubscribed, with a warning. No emails |
| An inbound email | Never subscribes an existing contact (opt-in or not); a new one is created unsubscribed |
| The dashboard's **Add contact**, a contact's switch, `PATCH /contacts/:id`, bulk subscribe, the end of a snooze | Subscribe at once: they are your own decision, or bring back a consent already given |
| Adding to a segment (`createMissing`) | Never subscribes: new contacts are created unsubscribed |

Sign up from your server with the secret key: anyone can read a public key in a page, and with it make your project email any address.

`/v1/track` answers `data.pendingConfirmation: true` when the sign-up waits, and `data.optIn` says what became of the email: `sent`, `throttled` (one went out a moment ago, or the contact's limits are spent), `capped` (the project's limits), `failed` (it could not be queued; the next sign-up tries again) or `held` (no email for this key). `POST /contacts` answers the same in `_meta`.

The `segments` of `/v1/track` and `/v1/send` are joined when the contact confirms, not before, so the workflows that start on their entry events run once the contact can receive them. The event of `/v1/track` is recorded at once: workflows that start on it run for a contact still waiting, and their marketing emails are skipped. Start welcome emails on `contact.subscribed` or on the segment's entry instead.

## The confirmation

The link opens a page that only shows what will happen; the contact confirms by pressing the button, so mail scanners that open links do not confirm anyone. Confirming subscribes the contact, fires `contact.subscribed` (with the confirmation email as its email, and no `reason`) and joins the segments of the sign-up.

A link keeps working until the contact confirms or a newer confirmation email replaces it. It no longer subscribes the contact once they unsubscribe or snooze, once their address changes, or once it bounces or is reported as spam.

## Limits

- One confirmation email per contact every 15 minutes, 3 a day, and none for 30 days after 3 in a row without a confirmation. A form sent twice sends one email.
- Confirmation emails to 500 contacts an hour and 5000 emails a day per project. Past that, or when an email cannot be sent (the sender's domain, a billing limit), new sign-ups still wait, without an email, and the project's failure alerts say so: check the sign-up form for bots.

## Finding who has not confirmed

The contacts list has a **Pending confirmation** status, and the filter of the same name lists them; they also count as **Unsubscribed**. A contact stays pending until it confirms, unsubscribes or is deleted, however long that takes. The contact's page says since when and when the last email went out, and the CSV export writes `pending` in its status column.

With that filter, the bulk actions take only the rows you tick, and the export button exports the list without the status filter.

Turning the double opt-in off leaves the pending contacts as they are: their links still confirm. It can be turned off while the sender's domain is not verified.
